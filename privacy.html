Learn Kanji: Pen-Enabled privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Learn Kanji: Pen-Enabled is a kanji writing practice app. It does not collect, store, transmit or share personal information.

  • Your writing, progress and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • When you tap a speaker button, your device's built-in speech feature reads the word aloud. Some voices provided by the browser or operating system may use an online service; the app does not control or receive that data.
  • Backups you save are files on your own device. The app does not upload them.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data removes everything the app stored.
